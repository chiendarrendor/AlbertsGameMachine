## MerchantOfVenus-specific

- [ ] Implement player-to-player trading (per the rulebook: a player ending their turn on a spaceport, city, or asteroid can exchange any amount of money/goods/relics/fares/equipment/IOUs — not ships or deeds — with another player occupying the same space, at any mutually-agreed price). Confirmed 2026-09-17 this is entirely unimplemented: none of `MerchantOfVenusServer.xml`'s 24 transitions are player-vs-player (`BUY`/`SELL`/`PICKUP`/`DROPOFF`/`JETTISON`/`TRADEINSHIP`/`REDEEMIOU` are all player-vs-`TradeBase`), no unwired helper exists on `Player`, and the client GUI has no trace of it either. **Candidate for deferral** — Albert doesn't recall ever seeing this rule used across all the in-person games he's played, similar in spirit to the combat ruleset being deferred by design.

- [x] ~~Validate buy/sell vs. barter behavior~~ RESOLVED 2026-09-13: confirmed correct. Money and barter are separate `Player` totals; barter is zeroed every `ENDTURN` while cash persists. Token disposal (`SellDestination`: CUP/CULTURE/NOWHERE) is a separate, orthogonal mechanism — all matches `docs/PurchaseNotes.txt` exactly.
